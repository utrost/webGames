# How to Play

This is the current control reference for Web Games Arcade. Every game can be played with mouse, keyboard, and touch.

Global controls:

- `Esc`: pause/resume in games that support pausing.
- `R`: restart when a game-over or puzzle-reset state supports it.
- `F3`: toggle the FPS/performance overlay.
- Back button: return to the arcade lobby.

## Cosmic Breaker

Goal: keep the ball in play, break all bricks, collect power-ups, and advance through the level set.

Mouse:

- Move the pointer horizontally to aim the paddle.
- Click to serve a waiting ball.

Keyboard:

- `Arrow Left` / `Arrow Right`: move paddle.
- `Space` or `Arrow Up`: serve a waiting ball.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.
- `R`: restart after game over.

Touch:

- Drag or hold a finger horizontally to position the paddle.
- Tap to serve a waiting ball.

## Neon Flow

Goal: rotate pipe tiles so each source routes the correct colored flow into matching receivers.

Mouse:

- Click a pipe tile to rotate it clockwise.

Keyboard:

- Arrow keys: move the grid cursor.
- `Space` or `Enter`: rotate the selected tile.
- `R`: restart the puzzle.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.

Touch:

- Tap a pipe tile to rotate it clockwise.

## Orbit

Goal: protect the sun by launching counter-bodies into incoming comets while keeping planets in useful orbits.

Mouse:

- Drag from a launch point and release to fire.
- The drag preview shows the launch direction.

Keyboard:

- Arrow keys: move the launch point.
- `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`: adjust launch direction.
- `Space`: fire.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.
- `R`: restart after game over.

Touch:

- Drag from a launch point and release to fire.

## Asteroids

Goal: survive asteroid waves, split and destroy rocks, and avoid collisions while inertia tries to make a fool of you.

Mouse:

- Hold the left third of the screen to rotate left.
- Hold the middle/top region to thrust.
- Hold the middle/bottom region to rotate right.
- Hold the right third of the screen to fire.

Keyboard:

- `Arrow Left` / `Arrow Right`: rotate.
- `Arrow Up`: thrust.
- `Space`: fire.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.
- `R`: restart after game over.

Touch:

- Left third: rotate left.
- Middle top: thrust.
- Middle bottom: rotate right.
- Right third: fire.
- Multiple simultaneous touches can combine actions.

## Starfall Armada

Goal: hold the defense line against a descending neon armada. Clear each formation before it reaches the breach line.

Mouse:

- Hold the lower-left region to move left.
- Hold the lower-right region to move right.
- Hold the lower-center region to fire.

Keyboard:

- `Arrow Left` / `Arrow Right`: move defender.
- `Space`: fire one active shot.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.
- `R`: restart after game over.

Touch:

- Lower left/right: move defender.
- Lower center: fire.

## Neon Blocks

Goal: stack tetrominoes, clear lines, use hold when useful, and survive as the drop speed increases.

Mouse:

- Click: rotate.
- Drag left/right: move.
- Drag down: soft drop or hard drop depending on drag distance.

Keyboard:

- `Arrow Left` / `Arrow Right`: move.
- `Arrow Down`: soft drop.
- `Arrow Up`: rotate.
- `Space`: hard drop.
- `C`: hold/swap the current piece.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.
- `R`: restart after game over.

Touch:

- Tap: rotate.
- Swipe left/right: move.
- Swipe down: soft drop or hard drop depending on swipe distance.

## Elemental Sandbox

Goal: paint elements and watch the falling-sand simulation create small messes. Sand, water, fire, oil, acid, plant, wood, stone, steam, and smoke all follow simple local rules.

Mouse:

- Drag on the canvas to paint.
- Use the element buttons and brush slider to choose material and brush size.

Keyboard:

- Number keys: select element.
- Arrow keys: move the brush cursor.
- `Space` or `Enter`: paint at the cursor.
- `[` / `]`: shrink/grow brush.
- `C` or `R`: clear the simulation.
- `Esc`: pause/resume.

Touch:

- Drag on the canvas to paint.
- Tap element buttons and use the brush slider to change tools.
